NEXUS Transaction Credit Table

           

 

Transaction Credits are purchased per calendar quarter in advance according to the expected transaction volumes of your business.  One (1) Transaction Credit equals one (1) message, document, data file sent.  Only messages that you send are charged for, there is no charge to receive messages, regardless of the transaction type.

However, a Nexus member may elect to pay for the messages received from a trading partner, for example in the case of a Supplier paying for Purchase Orders received from a Customer.  Please inform Nexus if this is the case so that the correct billing occurs.

 

The following rate table indicates the Transaction Credit categories for sending messages, documents or data via the Nexus B2B Network.

 

From the following table, estimate your monthly transaction volume of messages that your organisation will send via Nexus, and enter the number of Transaction Credits required per month at the end of this page. This value will be used for billing on a quarterly basis.

 

N.B.  Excess transaction credits purchased will be credited towards the following quarterly billing period, therefore it is best to overestimate the transaction volumes slightly and you will be notified of the actual transaction volume of credits used during the quarter.

 

You will be notified during the calendar quarter when the Transaction Credits are at 75% of the purchased quantity, then again when 90% have been used.  Additional Transaction Credits must be purchased at this time to avoid interruption of service and a re-connection fee.  When 100% of the Transaction Credits have been used in any calendar quarter, and additional Credits have not been purchased, your Nexus service may be discontinued until Credits have been purchased.
